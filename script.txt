Imagine a place in the universe where the rules of physics completely break down. A place so dense and powerful that not even light can escape its gravitational pull. These are black holes, the ultimate cosmic mysteries. When a massive star dies, it collapses in on itself, crushing matter into an infinitely small point. But what actually happens inside? Near the edge, known as the event horizon, time slows to a crawl. Beyond that, science has no answers. Matter vanishes, and secrets are trapped forever. Are black holes portals to other dimensions, or just the end of everything? We are only beginning to unlock their dark secrets.
